About

I'm Victor Benito, originally from Barcelona, currently based in Linz, Austria. My Master's thesis at the LIT AI Lab focuses on whether invariant learning methods (IRM, VREx) add meaningful value on top of large pretrained models like CLIP — tested on real-world distribution shift benchmarks.

I also work as a working student at Infineon Technologies, where I build AI-powered tools including a RAG system and an LLM-based code assistant.
